# Ode

## What it is
A personal portfolio for Kaleb Wright, published at github.com/kkaleb-w/ode and meant to live at a
personal domain. Not a resume page. It is a quiet, crafted place that a visitor wants to stay in.

## Who it is for
People who arrive from a GitHub link, a photography post, a conversation, or a job application:
recruiters, engineers, photographers, and friends. They arrive with no context and give it one
screen before deciding whether to keep looking.

## What it must do
- Present Kaleb's work as an experience, not a list: photography, software (Rosaria), code activity.
- Make the visitor feel a specific mood: calm, nocturnal, poetic, "outside breezy wind" — closer to
  a poem read slowly at night than to a product landing page.
- Prove real activity: live GitHub contribution history (the green graph is the point), real repos,
  real photographs. No invented metrics, no fake testimonials, no stock imagery.
- Work on a phone as a first-class experience, not a squeezed desktop.

## Truth constraints
- Kaleb Wright, kalebrwright@gmail.com, GitHub `kkaleb-w`, 3 public repos (`ode`, `national-park`,
  `portfolio`), Rosaria (github.com/kkaleb-w/Rosaria) is his main product: a Rosary PWA.
- All photography is his own. Any image on the site is either his photograph or an authored asset.
- No commercial claims, no client list, no pricing.

## Success
A visitor lands, understands within one screen that this is a person who makes quiet, deliberate
things, and leaves having opened at least two of the folders or the lit window.

## Brand commitments
- The site is an ode to the person who made it; it never explains itself, never sells, never
  performs enthusiasm. Restraint is the voice.
- Influences Kaleb named: John Keats (poetry), Zach Bryan (song), night photography.
- No gamification, no hype, no badge clutter, no analytics theater.
